## Brand & Style

This design system crafts a calm, scientifically rigorous, and contemplative environment for sleep health, circadian hygiene, and generative dream analysis. It reconciles precision neuro-monitoring with the introspective mystery of subconscious dreamscapes.

### Personality & Tone
- **Somnolent & Restorative:** The interface rejects jarring high-alert visuals, embracing low-strain deep navy atmospheres that promote melatonin synthesis and evening relaxation.
- **Clinical Precision:** Sleep architecture, macronutrient breakdowns, and circadian cycles are presented with disciplined clarity, structured telemetry, and unmistakable data hierarchy.
- **Ethereal & Cognitive:** Dream interpretation layers are illuminated with bioluminescent cyan and teal accents, evoking synaptic firing, neural networks, and nocturnal clarity.

### Visual Style
A hybrid of **Refined Frosted Glassmorphism** and **Contemporary Clinical HUD**. The canvas rests on atmospheric deep indigo gradients with abstract neural-constellation underlays. Information lives within pristine white-to-translucent cards and deep-slate containers that provide daylight legibility for medical data while preserving the serene, dark nocturnal aesthetic. Accents shimmer with cyan and soft amber warnings, delivering high contrast without sensory overload.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around nocturnal restfulness, circadian cues, and high-readability scientific displays:

- **Primary (`#00E5FF` - Bioluminescent Cyan):** Represents synaptic lucidity, AI processing states, active sleep stages (REM), and actionable touchpoints. Emits a soft, calming glow against midnight surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#1B3A5B` - Oceanic Navy):** Serves as mid-tier structural surfaces, subtle card containers, navigational tabs, and grounded UI structures.
- **Tertiary (`#FFB347` - Warm Amber/Melatonin):** Reserved for contextual hygiene alerts, nutritional stimulant warnings (caffeine, late meals), and circadian evening cues. Softened to avoid alarmist red tones that spike cortisol.
- **Neutral (`#0A1322` - Deep Midnight Obsidian):** The foundational canvas. Replaces pure sterile black with a deep chromatic blue-black that feels infinite, tranquil, and restorative.

### Surface Color Strategy
1. **Background Canvas:** Gradient flowing from `#0A1322` to `#0E233E` with faint cyan neural constellation overlays.
2. **Elevated Frosted Panels (Data Cards):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)` in high-density clinical data blocks for surgical legibility, or `rgba(18, 38, 64, 0.75)` for immersive dark cards with `1px` subtle cyan borders (`rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.2)`).
3. **Typography on Light Cards:** `#0D1C2E` (Deep Charcoal Blue) for high contrast and zero eye fatigue.
4. **Typography on Dark Canvas:** `#F0F6FC` (Starlight White) for headlines, `#94A3B8` (Slate Silver) for secondary metrics.

## Typography

The type system blends human empathy with clinical and technological precision:

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its friendly, rounded geometry and gentle optical curvature. It provides approachable warmth to complex neuro-clinical topics, reducing psychological friction.
- **Body Text (Manrope):** Delivers clean linear proportions, modern geometric clarity, and high legibility across extensive dream journaling entries and nutritional feedback.
- **Labels & Micro-data (Space Grotesk):** Provides a precise, technological cadence for telemetry metrics (mg of caffeine, REM ratios, timestamps, biometric parameters, AI model tags).

### Editorial Rules
- Metric values (e.g., `456.7 kcal`, `95 mg`) pair Space Grotesk tabular figures with medium weight to ensure alignment within clinical summary tables.
- Dream narratives use `body-lg` with generous line-height (`26px`) to promote comfortable long-form review and reflective writing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt spatial grid with generous negative space, mirroring the calm pacing of deep sleep.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Centered high-focus container constrained between 960px and 1140px. Two-column asynchronous layout for paired sections (such as Sleep Hygiene Habits paired alongside Nutritional Biometrics).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Adaptive 8-column layout; nutritional breakdowns and correlation cards collapse to sequential vertical stacks.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stacked workflow. Navigation condenses to a fixed bottom docked bar or top slim header. Input cards shift to full-width modules with horizontal scroll chip sets for emotion and symbol selectors.

### Rhythmic Padding
- Section cards maintain generous internal padding (`space-lg` or 24px) to avoid claustrophobic clinical interfaces.
- Data pairs and badges utilize tight horizontal clusters (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) to preserve relationship clarity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered luminescence and translucent substrate tiers:

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Atmospheric Canvas):** Deep indigo base (`#0A1322`) with low-opacity radial synaptic node networks (`#00E5FF` at 8% opacity with soft blur).
2. **Level 1 (Substrate Containers):** Subtle nocturnal cards constructed with frosted acrylic: `rgba(18, 38, 64, 0.45)`, a 16px backdrop-filter blur, and a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Level 2 (High-Contrast Focus Cards):** Pristine white cards (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.96)`) used for intensive data entry, nutritional summaries, and diagnostic output. These feature soft ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 15, 35, 0.28)`.
4. **Level 3 (Interactive Floats & Modals):** Pill-shaped action buttons and dream insight alerts sit on high elevation with a bioluminescent aura: `0 0 20px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.35)`.
5. **Warning & Alert Banners:** Tinted light cream-gold surfaces (`rgba(255, 248, 225, 0.95)`) bordered with subtle amber strokes (`#FFE082`), casting a warm, soft diffuse shadow.

## Shapes

The design uses roundedness tier **2** (0.5rem base, with 1rem for cards and rounded-full for interactive tokens):

- **Main Cards & Panels:** Bound by `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px), delivering a soft, non-threatening, organic silhouette reminiscent of modern medical tech hardware.
- **Input Fields & Form Selectors:** Styled with `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) with inset borders to anchor structured data entry.
- **Badges, Tags, & Status Pills:** Styled as fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), reinforcing their identity as interactive dream symbols or physiological status pills.
- **Progress & Telemetry Bars:** Rounded caps (`rounded-full`) that convey fluidity and circadian rhythm continuity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary AI Action Button:** Bioluminescent cyan backdrop (`#00E5FF`) with dark midnight typography (`#0A1322`), semi-bold Space Grotesk. Hover state initiates an ethereal outer glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.4)`, starlight white text. Hover fills to `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Card Action Pill:** White background with subtle slate border, housing an icon and micro-copy (e.g., "Re-analizar con IA", "Modificar / Siesta").

### Dream & Emotion Chips (Interactive Badges)
- Pill-shaped tags (`rounded-full`), height: 28px, Space Grotesk 12px.
- **Selected State:** Dark teal-cyan fill (`#0E4968`), high-contrast cyan border (`#00E5FF`), starlight text.
- **Unselected State:** Translucent navy (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), muted slate text (`#94A3B8`).

### Form Inputs & Textareas
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** Pure white or frosted-slate background, crisp `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, 44px touch height, smooth focus ring with `0 0 0 2px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.4)`.
- **Dream Journaling Textarea:** Generous min-height (140px), comfortable line height (`24px`), subtle placeholder text guiding emotional recall and somatic sensations.

### Metric Cards & Progress Bars
- **Nutritional & Biometric Micro-Cards:** White glossy background, split into symmetrical metric boxes (Calories, Lipids, Proteins, Carbs). Large tabular Space Grotesk values with color-coded labels (e.g., Caffeine highlighted in tertiary amber).
- **Circadian Telemetry Bars:** Thin (6px) progress tracks with smooth cyan-to-indigo gradients indicating sleep cycles (NREM, Deep, REM).

### Recommendations & AI Insight Panels
- Cards feature numbered circular badges (`w-6 h-6`, cyan background, navy bold numeral) followed by actionable sleep hygiene rules.
- Card borders use a subtle dual-tone gradient outline to indicate AI-assisted generation.
